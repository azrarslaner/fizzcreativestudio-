Two people. One studio. A lot of fizz

Fizz started with a simple idea. Small businesses deserve the same smart branding that big brands get, without the big-agency price tag or the big-agency attitude.

So we put two very different minds in one room: a psychologist who studies why people buy, and a developer who builds the places where they do. One of us understands the customer’s mind, the other builds the customer’s journey. Together we make brands that look good and work.

We work online with cafés, restaurants, clinics, salons, hotels and shops that are ready to be noticed.

One of us finds out why people choose you.

Göktuğ

Psychologist · Brand & Growth Strategist

Göktuğ is a psychologist who spends his days asking one question: why do people choose what they choose?

He has studied and trained in consumer psychology, consumer behaviour, neuromarketing and the behavioural sciences: how attention works, what builds trust, why one menu sells more than another, and what makes someone stop scrolling. He knows the small signals that turn a passer-by into a regular.

He also does the hands-on work. Göktuğ has experience in graphic design, SEO and paid advertising, so the strategy doesn’t stay on paper. He designs it, launches it and measures it.

  • Consumer psychology
  • Neuromarketing
  • Behavioural science
  • Graphic design
  • SEO
  • Paid ads

The other makes sure they can find you.

Azra

Developer · Web & Digital Lead

Azra is a software developer who turns ideas into things that work, from the website your customers land on to the booking and menu pages behind it.

She has training and experience in mobile development, digital marketing and web-based content management systems. She builds sites that load fast and look right on every phone. When your menu, prices or photos change, you send them to us and we update the site for you.

Because she also knows digital marketing, she builds with results in mind: pages that are easy to find on Google, simple to book from and ready for your ads to send people to.

  • Mobile development
  • Content management systems
  • Digital marketing
  • Google-ready pages
  • Menus & booking
