How we work

First, over coffee. We meet you, in person or on a call, and look at your place the way your customers do. Göktuğ works out why people choose you. Azra checks where they find you.

Then, one presentation. Logo, colours, voice and a first set of posts, shown together so you see the whole brand at once, not in pieces.

Launch day. New sign, new feed, new website. Everything goes live together, so nobody meets the old you by accident.

Every month after. We keep posting, running the ads and reading the numbers, and we change what isn’t working.

Good questions

Can I start with just one thing, like a logo?
Yes. Pick one service or all four. A lot of clients start with the brand and add the feed or the website later.
Do you only work in Prishtina and Istanbul?
That’s where we are, not where we stop. We work online with businesses wherever they are, and visit when the work needs it.
What does the first meeting cost?
Nothing. The first consultation is free. We listen, look at what you have and tell you honestly what we’d do first.
Who updates the website after launch?
We do. When your menu, prices or photos change, send them to us and we update the site for you.
Will we talk to the people doing the work?
Always. Fizz is two people, so the ones you meet are the ones who design, write and build it.
